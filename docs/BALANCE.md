# Balance report

Generated by `dev/balance_report.py` from the game's own data. *Story danger* is the game's recommended level for the chapters you'd be on. *Enemies* is the median HP multiplier on random enemies of the areas you'd be in (home danger within 3 of the story's) / of older areas (6+ below), which are raised on purpose. Every preset opens as vanilla and reaches the values in overhaul.ini by the mid game (stage ramp). *Chapter boss* is the HP / Atk multiplier on a boss of the chapter you'd be doing, fought then.
